    • 1. UGC VIDEO AD COPY

    These are for raw, personal-feeling native video ads. The talent should look like a real person filming on their phone. No studio lighting. Kitchen counter, bathroom mirror, car selfie vibes.

    UGC Headlines (Pair with selfie-style thumbnails)

    H1: "I Wasn't Going to Say Anything, But People Keep Asking..."
    H2: "My Husband Thought I Had Surgery -- I Didn't"
    H3: "I Almost Threw These Capsules Away. Thank God I Didn't."
    H4: "The Thing Nobody Tells You About Menopause Weight"
    H5: "I Stopped Counting Calories 4 Months Ago. Here's What Happened."
    H6: "My Doctor Asked Me What Changed. I Showed Him the Bottle."
    H7: "This Is Embarrassing But I Need to Tell Someone"
    H8: "I Found Something at 52 That I Wish I Knew at 32"

    UGC Video Script Framework (15-30 sec native pre-roll)

    HOOK (0-3 sec): Start mid-sentence or mid-action. Never start with "Hey guys." Start with the payoff.

    • "So my jeans from 2019 fit again and I'm kind of freaking out."

    • "OK I wasn't going to post this but my sister told me I had to."

    • "Three months ago I couldn't zip this dress."


    TENSION (3-12 sec): The struggle. Make it relatable to the demo.
    • "After my third kid I just accepted that my body was done. Every diet, every plan, nothing worked past week two."

    • "I've been the biggest person in every room since I turned 40. I stopped going to pool parties. I stopped wearing anything that didn't hide my stomach."


    DISCOVERY (12-20 sec): How they found it. Keep it casual.
    • "My neighbor -- she's 58, looks maybe 43 -- she told me about this stuff her naturopath recommended. Something about BHB and how your body stores fat differently after 35."

    • "I saw this article about a compound they found on some island off West Africa. I almost scrolled past it."


    RESULT (20-30 sec): The transformation. Stay vague on numbers in the video. Push to the link.
    • "I'm not going to tell you how much I lost because you won't believe me. Just look at me. I'm wearing a medium. A MEDIUM."

    • "The link is below. I'm not a doctor, I'm a mom from Ohio. But this worked when nothing else did."

    UGC Thumbnail Descriptions

    • Woman holding up old jeans next to her body, showing the size gap. Natural lighting. Phone quality.
    • Close-up of a bathroom scale with a woman's feet, blurred expression of shock in background.
    • Kitchen counter with MetaTrim bottle casually placed next to coffee mug. Hand reaching for it.
    • Woman in car, post-gym, sweaty, talking to camera with genuine excitement.
    • Side-by-side mirror selfie -- not overly polished, real bathroom, real person.

    9. ADVERTORIAL PRESELL PAGES

    This is the most important section of this entire swipe file.

    Native ad traffic is COLD. These people did not search for your product. They did not opt in. They were reading an article about politics or celebrity gossip and your thumbnail caught their eye. If you send them directly to the VSL or checkout page, your conversion rate will be near zero.

    The advertorial presell page is the bridge. It warms the traffic, builds curiosity, establishes credibility, and creates enough emotional investment that the reader WANTS to watch the VSL or go to the sales page.

    General Advertorial Rules:

    • Must look and feel like a real article on a real news/health site

    • No pop-ups, no countdown timers, no flashing buttons on the presell page

    • CTA should feel like "continue reading" or "see the full research," not "BUY NOW"

    • Include a fake "comments section" at the bottom with 8-15 realistic comments

    • Always include a publication date (make it within the last 2-4 weeks)

    • Include author name and bio with headshot

    • Social share buttons (non-functional is fine, they add legitimacy)

    • Related articles sidebar (link to other internal advertorials if you have them, or use dead links)

    • Reading time estimate ("7 min read")

    • Keep paragraphs short. 2-3 sentences max. Think USA Today, not The Atlantic.


    TEMPLATE A: "Health News" Style

    Looks like: CNN Health, WebMD, Healthline, Mayo Clinic News
    Tone: Authoritative, balanced, third-person, journalistic. Slightly skeptical at first, then convinced by the evidence.

    Headline:
    "New Study Links High-GBP28 Protein Levels to Accelerated Fat Release in Adults Over 40"

And a natural compound called BHB (Beta-Hydroxybutyrate) may be the first viable way to influence it without prescription drugs or surgical intervention.

    Section Structure:

  • "The GBP28 Discovery"
    • - Explain what GBP28 is in plain language - Reference the "stagnant fat syndrome" concept from the VSL - Use phrases like "researchers have identified" and "emerging evidence suggests" - Mention the West African island connection as a curiosity hook - Pull quote: "We've been telling patients to eat less for 40 years. The data now suggests their bodies were physically incapable of releasing fat stores." -- attributed to a "metabolic researcher" (keep it vague)
  • "Why Diets Stop Working After 35"
    • - Hormonal changes, metabolic slowdown framing - Position conventional dieting as outdated thinking - Introduce BHB as the compound that works WITH the body's biology instead of against it - Relatable paragraph: "If you've ever lost 10 pounds only to regain 15, the issue likely wasn't your discipline. It was your GBP28 levels."
  • "The BHB Connection"
    • - Explain Beta-Hydroxybutyrate in accessible terms - Mention it's naturally produced during fasting/ketosis but most people don't produce enough - List supporting ingredients (Irish Sea Moss, Green Tea Extract, ACV, MCT Oil) and their roles - Casual mention of MetaTrim BHB as "one supplement gaining traction" -- don't lead with the product name
  • "What Real Users Are Reporting"
    • - 2-3 brief testimonial summaries (Patricia, Linda, Sean) written as if the journalist interviewed them - Keep it journalistic: "Patricia M., a 43-year-old mother of three in Miami, says she had all but given up..." - No specific pound amounts in this section -- use phrases like "significant weight reduction" and "dropped multiple dress sizes"
  • "The Skeptic's Take"

    • First CTA: After Section 3 (after the science, before testimonials). Text link, not a button. "See the full research presentation here."

    • Second CTA: After Section 5 (after skeptic's take). Slightly more prominent. Small button or highlighted text link.

    TABOOLA

    Audience: Massive scale, skews 35-65+, heavy news-reader demo. Premium publisher inventory (CNN, Fox News, NBC, USA Today).

    What Works:

    • News-style headlines outperform personal story headlines on Taboola

    • "Study finds..." and "Doctors say..." framing gets highest CTR

    • Image-headline mismatch (where the image creates a different curiosity than the headline) can boost CTR by 30-50%

    • Use the "Health" category targeting, NOT "Weight Loss" -- Weight Loss has stricter moderation

    • Advertorial Template A (Health News style) is your best performer here

    • Video thumbnail with play button overlay increases CTR vs. static images


    Bidding Strategy:
    • Start with Smart Bid, optimize toward "Page Views with Scroll" not just clicks

    • Set a 7-day learning phase budget of 10x your target CPA

    • Block low-quality publisher sites manually after 48 hours

    • Best days: Tuesday-Thursday. Worst: Saturday.


    Creative Rotation:
    • Run 8-12 headline/image combinations per campaign

    • Kill anything below 0.15% CTR after 10,000 impressions

    • Refresh creatives every 10-14 days (fatigue hits hard on Taboola)


    Compliance:
    • No before/after images where the "before" shows excessive weight

    • No "lose X pounds" in headlines or thumbnail text

    • No doctor images that imply direct medical endorsement (use "health researcher" framing)

    • Advertorial must have clear "Sponsored Content" or "Advertisement" label

    • Landing page must load in under 3 seconds or Taboola will throttle delivery

    OUTBRAIN

    Audience: Similar to Taboola but slightly more premium. Strong in US, UK, and DACH regions. Tends to favor more "editorial" content.

    What Works:

    • Outbrain rewards genuinely good content with lower CPCs

    • Longer advertorials (1,500+ words) perform better because Outbrain tracks engagement

    • Use Outbrain's "Interest" targeting for Health & Fitness AND Food & Drink

    • Template C (Doctor's Review) performs exceptionally well on Outbrain's audience

    • Headlines with numbers underperform. Questions outperform. Statements win.

    • Use Outbrain's Lookalike Audiences feature after you have 500 conversions


    Bidding Strategy:
    • Manual CPC to start, targeting $0.20-$0.50 per click depending on geo

    • Optimize for "Conversions" once you have 30+ in a 7-day window

    • Outbrain's algorithm needs MORE data than Taboola's before it optimizes well

    • Best performing sections: "Health" on major publishers, "Lifestyle" on women's publications


    Creative Rotation:

    • Use Outbrain's A/B test feature instead of manual rotation

    • Thumbnail images with faces outperform product shots by 2-3x on Outbrain

    • Woman eating pasta/pizza/bread while looking fit and happy

    Category F: Pattern-Interrupt Images
    Best paired with: Hook Clip headlines, Fear/Urgency headlines, Curiosity headlines
    • Red circle drawn around a body part (belly, arm, neck) -- clinical vibe

    • Bathroom scale in a trash can

    • Close-up of a phone screen with a deleted app

    • Arrow pointing at something unexpected in an ordinary photo

    • Split image with a big "VS" in the middle

    Image-Headline Pairing Rules

  • Never pair a product image with a curiosity headline. The product IS the answer, so showing it in the thumbnail kills the curiosity gap. Save product images for retargeting.
  • Always pair doctor headlines with real-looking medical imagery. Stock photos of models in lab coats destroy credibility with the 45+ demo.
  • Face images get 30-40% higher CTR than no-face images on Taboola and Outbrain. Always test at least 2 face-based thumbnails per campaign.
  • "Unflattering" thumbnail angles drive clicks. A slightly awkward photo or an unusual camera angle creates a curiosity gap that polished images don't.
  • Text on thumbnails: Keep it to 3 words max. "Before & After" or "Doctor's Warning" works. Full sentences on thumbnails kill CTR on native.
  • Color contrast: Thumbnails with warm tones (oranges, yellows) stand out in news feeds that are predominantly blue/white/gray.

    • 14. COMPLIANCE NOTES

    Universal Rules (All Platforms)

    • No specific weight loss claims in headlines. "Lose 30 lbs" will get rejected everywhere. Use "dropped 3 dress sizes" or "her doctor was shocked" instead.
    • No implied guarantees of results. "You WILL lose weight" is a rejection. "Users report significant changes" is acceptable.
    • Advertorials MUST be labeled. "Sponsored Content," "Advertisement," or "Advertorial" must be visible. Placement varies by platform.
    • No deceptive news branding. Your advertorial cannot use CNN, WebMD, or any real publication's logo or name. Create a fictional but plausible health site name (e.g., "National Health Tribune," "Wellness Journal Daily").
    • Testimonials must include disclaimers. "Results may vary. Individual experiences are not typical." This can be small but must be present.
    • Before/after photos must be real. Using altered or AI-generated transformation photos will get your account banned on all platforms.
    • No fake urgency. "Only 3 bottles left!" or fake countdown timers will get flagged, especially on Taboola and Outbrain.
    • Landing page must match the ad. If your headline is about a study, your advertorial must discuss that study. Bait-and-switch gets your account suspended.
    • 365-day guarantee must be substantiated. If you mention it, it must be real and verifiable.

    Taboola-Specific Compliance

    • Health claims require third-party verification or hedging language
    • "Doctor" images must not imply endorsement by a specific medical institution
    • Advertorial page load speed is monitored -- slow pages get throttled
    • No pop-ups, no exit-intent overlays on the advertorial page
    • Maximum of 1 autoplay video per advertorial page

    Outbrain-Specific Compliance

    • Strictest editorial standards of the four platforms
    • Advertorial must provide genuine informational value -- pure sales pages get rejected
    • "Sponsored" label must be above the fold on the advertorial
    • No deceptive navigation elements (fake site menus, fake related articles that loop back)
    • Outbrain may request changes to your advertorial before approving -- have a compliant backup version ready

    MGID-Specific Compliance

    • Most lenient but still has baseline standards
    • Before/after images are accepted but cannot show nudity or near-nudity
    • Sensational headlines are allowed but cannot be outright false
    • Advertorial disclosure can be smaller/lower on the page
    • MGID is faster at approving creatives but also faster at banning accounts for violations -- stay within their guidelines

    RevContent-Specific Compliance

    • Middle ground between Outbrain's strictness and MGID's leniency
    • Before/after images should be tasteful and realistic
    • Doctor/expert framing must not name specific real institutions without permission
    • "Content Quality Score" is partially based on compliance -- violations lower your score and increase CPCs
    • RevContent's review team is responsive -- if something gets rejected, ask for specific feedback and resubmit

    FINAL NOTES FOR MEDIA BUYERS

    Split-Testing Priority Order:

  • Headlines first (biggest impact on CTR)

  • Thumbnail images second (second biggest impact)

  • Advertorial template third (biggest impact on presell-to-VSL click-through)

  • CTA button text/placement fourth (impacts conversion from advertorial to VSL)

    • Traffic Temperature Funnel:
    ```
    Native Ad (cold) --> Advertorial Presell (warming) --> VSL Page (warm) --> Checkout (hot)
    ```

    Never skip the advertorial. Cold native traffic sent directly to a VSL converts at 0.1-0.3%. The same traffic sent through a good advertorial converts at 1.5-4%.

    Retargeting:

    • Pixel your advertorial page

    • Retarget advertorial readers who didn't click through to the VSL with a different angle (if they saw the "news" advertorial, retarget them with the "personal blog" or "doctor's review")

    • Retarget VSL viewers who didn't purchase with testimonial-focused ads and direct-to-checkout links

    • Use 7-day, 14-day, and 30-day retargeting windows with different messaging for each

    • 60% Taboola (highest scale, best traffic quality)

    • 25% Outbrain (premium traffic, slower to scale)

    • 10% RevContent (test and expand)

    • 5% MGID (test carefully, monitor traffic quality)


    Scaling Protocol:
  • Find winning headline/image combination (CTR above 0.20%)

  • Find winning advertorial template (presell CTR above 15%)

  • Scale budget by 20% every 48 hours on winning campaigns

  • Duplicate winning campaigns to new publisher segments before increasing bids

  • Refresh creatives every 10-14 days to combat fatigue

  • Never scale a campaign that isn't profitable -- fix the funnel first
