## Facebook Compliance Dos and Don'ts

### FOR METATRIM BHB ADS SPECIFICALLY

---

### DO:

- **Frame as wellness support**, not as a medical treatment or cure
- **Use first-person stories** -- "I noticed more energy" is compliant; "You WILL have more energy" is not
- **Talk about the ingredients** -- BHB ketones, Irish Sea Moss, etc. are factual and safe to mention
- **Highlight the guarantee** -- 365-day money-back is a strong trust signal and always compliant
- **Use "may help," "can support," "designed to"** -- conditional language protects you
- **Include disclaimers** -- "Individual results vary" / "Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease" / "Consult your physician"
- **Reference general wellness benefits** -- energy, mental clarity, appetite balance, feeling good
- **Use social proof** -- real testimonials (adapted for compliance), star ratings, review counts
- **Show the product** -- bottle, capsules, label close-ups are all fine
- **Mention certifications** -- GMP, Made in USA, no gluten, no caffeine, no stimulants

### DON'T:

- **Never imply the viewer has a condition** -- "If you're overweight..." or "Struggling with obesity?" will get flagged immediately
- **Never use specific numbers in the ad text** -- "Lose 28 lbs" or "Drop 3 dress sizes" will get your ad rejected and possibly your account restricted
- **Never show before/after body images** -- side-by-side body comparisons are explicitly banned on Meta for health products
- **Never use the word "cure"** -- or "treat," "heal," "fix" in relation to any medical condition
- **Never guarantee specific results** -- "You WILL lose weight" is a policy violation
- **Never reference BMI, obesity, or clinical terms** -- these imply diagnosis
- **Never use scare tactics** -- "Your metabolism is BROKEN" or "You're DESTROYING your body" will get flagged
- **Never target based on health conditions** -- Meta's targeting policies prohibit this, and your ad copy shouldn't imply you're doing it
- **Never sell directly** -- frame it as "learn more about this approach" rather than "buy this weight loss pill now"
- **Never use deceptive thumbnails** -- no fake play buttons, no fake "news" headers that look like real publications without clearly marking as sponsored/advertorial
- **Never use the word "diet pill"** -- or "weight loss pill" or "fat burner" in ad copy. Use "wellness capsule" or "BHB supplement" or "metabolic support"

### LANDING PAGE COMPLIANCE:

- The landing page can be more direct than the ad, but still must comply with Meta's policies
- If Meta reviews the landing page and finds claims that contradict ad policy, the ad will be rejected
- Ensure the landing page has visible disclaimers, a privacy policy link, and terms of service
- Avoid auto-playing video with sound on the landing page (increases bounce and can trigger policy flags)

### AD ACCOUNT HEALTH:

- Start with lower daily budgets ($20-50/day) and scale up as ads are approved
- If an ad is rejected, DO NOT just resubmit the same copy. Fix the violation first
- Maintain a mix of ad types (educational, testimonial, product-focused) to avoid pattern-based flags
- Keep your ad relevance score high by testing creative regularly
- Use the Facebook Ad Library to check what competitors are running and staying compliant with
- If your ad account gets restricted, appeal with documentation showing your compliance efforts

## TESTING FRAMEWORK

**Phase 1 -- Hook Testing (Week 1-2)**
Run 5-8 hook variations against the same body copy and creative. Kill anything under 3% CTR after 1,000 impressions.

**Phase 2 -- Body Copy Testing (Week 2-3)**
Take winning hooks and test 3-4 different body copy approaches (UGC, testimonial, advertorial, lifestyle). Track CPA and ROAS.

**Phase 3 -- Creative Testing (Week 3-4)**
Pair winning copy combos with different video/image styles. Test UGC video vs. product animation vs. lifestyle imagery.

**Phase 4 -- Audience Expansion (Week 4+)**
Scale winning combinations. Test broad targeting vs. interest-based. Test LAL audiences based on purchasers.

- CTR (Link): Target 2%+
- CPA: Track against AOV ($271.83)
- Hook Rate (video): First 3 seconds, target 30%+
- Hold Rate: 15-second mark, target 15%+
- ROAS: Target 3x+ for cold traffic
