Key Ingredients

Calcium BHB

Beta-Hydroxybutyrate (BHB) is an exogenous ketone that helps your body enter ketosis faster. It provides an immediate source of clean energy for your brain and muscles.

Studies show BHB can help reduce appetite, increase mental clarity, and accelerate fat burning even when you're not in a strict ketogenic state.

Irish Sea Moss

A red seaweed found on the coast of Ireland, Irish Sea Moss is packed with 92 essential minerals your body needs.

It provides calcium, iron, potassium, magnesium, zinc, and copper. During the Great Irish Famine, people survived by eating this nutritious seaweed. It supports thyroid health, gut health, and heart health.
